# Start useful work with Caiman

You can work on a product before you have sales history or an Amazon connection.

## Your first useful sequence

1. **Set up one business folder.** Install Caiman Core. Add the complete VIP or
   GLS+ kit from your membership, including its download verification. If that
   download is unavailable, ask member support for it; Core alone is not the kit.
2. **Save the facts.** Add your product details, current listing, reference images
   and preferences. Caiman saves them in this business's local memory.
3. **Prepare the first improvement.** Ask for a listing review and image ideas.
   Caiman uses your facts and identifies assumptions. No sales report is required.
4. **Review the output.** Open **What to fix** in the dashboard. Review proposed
   changes and use **Download PDF**. These are proposals; nothing is applied to Amazon.
5. **Choose one next action.** Resolve an unsupported claim, revise one image
   concept, or add the next missing report. Caiman saves the next step for your return.

Start with:

> Help me set up my business with this kit and these product files. Amazon is not
> connected yet. Remember my product details and preferences, show me what to work
> on, and give me a PDF of the proposed listing changes and image ideas.

Caiman prepares a one-time Project-instruction block. Copy it into this
business Project's settings, keep existing instructions, and tell the assistant
when saved so it can check the setting.

## Return without starting over

Open the same Project and folder. Say:

> I am back. Use my saved product details and preferences. Show me the review we
> prepared and the next useful step.

Dashboard buttons open saved views and files. A missing report or unfinished
proposal is shown as unavailable. Continuing analysis happens with your assistant
in the Project; the dashboard does not dispatch a background job.

When performance data becomes available, add the relevant reports or choose the
connected workflow for your existing tier. Caiman keeps missing data separate
from zero. Account changes and schedules require their own approval.
